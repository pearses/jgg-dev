About Just Good Grips

Just Good Grips is an Australian-owned tennis brand delivering premium overgrips at a price that makes changing them a non-issue. Founded in Adelaide, we're built on the belief that players shouldn't have to think twice about replacing their grip.

Standard overgrips at major retailers run $4–$7 each — so a pack of 12 costs upwards of $60. Just Good Grips brings that down to around $2.33 per grip, making it practical to change grips as often as a player should.

Welcome to the JGG Club Partnership

We're inviting a small number of Adelaide tennis clubs to partner with us this summer. This isn't a sales arrangement — it's a genuine partnership built around your club's competitive teams.

We provide your club with free grip supply for your top players, plus a small retail allocation to sell to members. No upfront payment. No ongoing commitment to start.

How the Program Works

1
We drop off or ship 4 packs of 60 grips to your club within the next few days. No payment required.
2
2 packs are for your competitive teams — no strings attached. Hand them to your top players and let them use them freely.
3
2 packs are retail stock for your pro shop or club desk. Sell them to members at RRP — your club keeps the profit.
4
After 90 days, pay the wholesale price ($120 each) for any retail packs sold, and return any unsold retail stock. That's it.

You only ever pay for retail stock you've already sold. The team packs are JGG's investment in your club, with no expectation of payment or reciprocal advertising.

Why We're Doing This

Grip prices at major retailers have made regular replacement feel like a luxury. We've seen players tape over worn grips, lose games in the last set because of slippage, and coaches fight for equipment budgets that barely stretch. That's the problem Just Good Grips was built to solve.

By partnering with clubs directly, we put grips in the hands of competitive players before they hit the pro shop — and your club earns something for stocking them. It's a better model for everyone.

What We Ask

  • Sell retail stock at RRP only. We protect consistent pricing across all partner clubs and cannot allow undercutting.
  • Keep team supply grips for your competitive players — not for resale or general distribution.
  • Be open to a restock conversation once the first batch sells through.
  • No social media requirement. Mention us if it feels natural; don't if it doesn't.
  • Do not share or forward this document to other parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if we don't sell the retail packs in 90 days?
Return them — no cost, no issue. You only pay for what you've sold.
Can we sell grips individually from the 60-packs?
Yes. Individual sales can increase your margin significantly. Get in touch and we'll work through pricing with you.
What if the teams go through their free packs quickly?
Let us know and we can discuss a restock ahead of the 90-day mark.
Is there an ongoing commitment?
None. This is a trial. If it works for your club, we continue. If not, we part ways with no hard feelings.
Who do we contact for questions or restocking?
Scott directly: 0468 331 309 or contact@justgoodgrips.com. No waiting, no ticketing system.
Can the team packs be used in training or comp?
Entirely your call. They're yours — use them however best supports your players.
